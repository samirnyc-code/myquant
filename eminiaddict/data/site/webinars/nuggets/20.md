**In short:** The systematic, rules-based version of the gap-fill trade. Run every morning through a yes/no filter; if all answers are yes you have a tradeable fill. The entry is a REVERSAL into the fill caused by a real level (50%, extension's 50%, daily pivot, prior S/R — micro break last). Prioritize the entry by the three retracement strategies: **first test > second test > micros.** Gap fills are an INCOME trade (fast, reliable), not a wealth strategy — and there's always another one tomorrow.

## The morning filter (all must be YES)
1. Is it NOT opt-ex Friday / futures rollover / first day of a month or quarter?
2. Are we inside 8:00–9:30am ET (the 90-min window between the Globex/8:00 & 8:30 volume marks and the bell)?
3. Is price within the **10-point gap-and-go level** of the 4pm close? (Above the close for a gap-up-to-fill, below for a gap-down-to-fill.)
4. Has price hit a real S/R level that gives a micro series of measured moves toward the fill?
→ All yes = tradeable fill. Any no = no trade.

## The 10-point rule (from the pit)
- Pit traders' psychological line: >10 handles from the 4pm close and they won't attempt the fill; inside it they defend it overnight.
- If price has already touched the 10-pt level, that touch is usually the high/low of the morning — trade the series from there into the fill (very profitable).
- Entries should be **≥5 points from the fill** (room to profit; a 2-pt fill is under-participated) and **≤10 points**. Exception: opening near another significant level (e.g. a big 50% short above the 10-pt line) can still act as the resistance that leads the fill.
- Tools needed, nothing more: 4pm-close line, ±10-pt gap lines, daily pivot, Fib.

## Entry priority — trade the CAUSE
- The three retracement entries rank first-test > second-test > micros. **Micros are last resort** (smallest, least-participated part of the market).
- Most fills enter on the **second test** (front-run the traded highs/lows) — a low-risk entry, no reduced-risk needed, target = the fill.
- Always prefer the EXACT 50% (the "cause") — that's the level programs defend, add to, fight for. Micros get shaken out if price hasn't moved far enough from the entry.

## When gaps DON'T fill (as important as when they do)
- **If a micro series toward the fill fails in the last 15 min before the bell, do NOT try a new entry** — those trapped fillers become fuel for a gap-and-go. Keeping an EARLIER position on is fine; chasing a late micro is not.
- Not in the fill in the last 15 min? The open's volatility will likely take you out — wait.
- A broken fill can still fill LATER: after the trend breaks (61.8% line / high-tick / low-tick signal), a new series confirms and gives a fresh entry back toward the gap. "One door closes, another opens."

## Trend context
- Gap fills WITH the major trend are far more powerful: in measured-move shorts trade gap-UPS (sell into the fill = continuation); in measured-move longs trade gap-DOWNS.
- Fills fill because participants have traded them for years — a self-fulfilling prophecy. It's the most-participated event in ES. Miss today's? There's another tomorrow.
