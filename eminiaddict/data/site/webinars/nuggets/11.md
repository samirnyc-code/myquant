**In short:** The written rule-set for trading the Euro: account rules (limits, stops, targets, 1% risk), the two tradeable sessions (European open + NY-open-to-European-close), the daily-level protocol (first test, front-run 4, step away after a stop-out), and when scaling is allowed (never on daily levels).

## Account rules
- ES = income trades; Euro = WEALTH-CREATION trades (192-pip ATR; daily setups carry 100–400-pip targets, ~once a week — like catching 100 ES points a week).
- Never ADD MONEY to a trading account — an account is grown, not topped up. If it's sliding, the problem is one of three: not using limit orders, over-trading, or not prioritizing the highest reward/risk trades.
- Never place a trade without a stop, and never blame the stop — "blame the entry." Never place a trade without a target (the −23.6% defines the reward/risk; the smaller the target, the lower the trade's priority).
- Enter at 50% retracements in the direction of the trend — price is the only constant across timeframes (indicators shift per timeframe; the 50% level is the same on micro, 15-min, daily).
- Limit orders only. A market order buys a short-term overbought / sells a short-term oversold — the worst possible fill.
- Max 1% risk per trade (find your personal "sweet spot" via a trading journal; never trade scared money).
- Don't sell highs / buy lows just because price is at S/R — wait for the 61.8% technical failure. ONE exception: a true DAILY-level 50% setup — the first test is always taken.
- A candle PIERCING through a 61.8% line on any timeframe invalidates the trade and marks a trend change.
- Trade light Mondays (the week's trend is being identified) and Fridays (bank profits; hedging/revenge flow, thin afternoons); light/no trades on option-expiration Fridays and rollover Thursdays — daily-chart setups excepted.

## Sessions (the backbone of the webinar)
- Session 1: **2:30–3:30 am ET** — the European open ±30 min. ~90% of the year's swing trades originate here. The Euro open "trusts" the trend — it continues it.
- Session 2: **8 am–noon ET** — hour before the NY bell to the European close. The NY open tends to COUNTER-trend.
- Noon–2:30 am ET = manage positions only; no new trades in the last hour of NY; expect the Asian session to take out late-day entries. If the Asian session stops you out — do NOTHING until Europe opens.

## Daily-level protocol
- Daily setups appear ~once a week and are identifiable 24–36 hours ahead (sometimes 1–2 weeks). They are the priority — everything else is noise around them.
- 16-pip stop, level front-run by 4 (typical). Reward/risk commonly 10:1–30:1, so you take the FIRST TEST blind — waiting for confirmation means chasing.
- Not filled at the 50? Front-run the previous highs/lows by 4. Stopped out (either way)? STEP AWAY — re-enter only after the 15-minute trend fails and gives a new setup.
- NO SCALING on daily levels (it halves the reward/risk). Rule of thumb: −23.6% target > 30 pips → all-in/all-out; < 30 pips → scale (16-pip stop, +6 first target, stop to −6 = free trade).
- On a daily swing: keep the full 16-pip stop until structure justifies moving it (a 15-minute measured move hits its −23.6%, or protection forms at a 50%) — then break-even / trail the 61.8s. Prefer NEW positions at new setups over averaging into the old one.

## Micros and other pairs
- Micros = lowest-priority trades, front-run 2–4, always in the direction of the 15-min/daily; reducing risk via the 61.8% stop does NOT license a bigger size ("you're reducing risk — keep it reduced").
- British pound = a 15-MINUTE vehicle (its micros are choppy, don't use them); spot charts: 5-minute is the smallest usable view — for real micros use 6E futures 233-tick.
- Don't map ES setups onto the Euro or vice versa — the correlation regime changes (they detached in December after trading tick-for-tick all 2009).
