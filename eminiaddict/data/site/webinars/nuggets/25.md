**In short:** How to tell a real bull-market resumption from a dead-cat bounce / the next leg down of a bear market — using ONE signal, repeated across a century of crashes (1930, 1937, 1973, 1987, 2000, 2008, 2020). **Wall Street always gets its fill in a full 50% retracement.** Whether that halfway-back squeeze continues up or reverses is decided by the EXTENSION: while the extension long level defends, you keep making highs; the moment it breaks, you've confirmed the top and the next full-50% short down. News (QE, rate cuts, jobless claims) is noise — only price matters.

## The repeating anatomy of every major top
1. Selloff → market squeezes **all the way back up into a full 50% retracement short** (the dead-cat bounce). "Wall Street always gets its fill."
2. Into that resistance: traditional longs → blow through target → **extension** long level (same-anchor-to-new-highs). The extension is the bullish line-in-the-sand.
3. **Extension DEFENDS → keep grinding to new highs. Extension BREAKS its 61.8% → reversal confirmed.** That break marks the high of the market.
4. Break → immediately squeeze into the opposing 50% short from highs; it may take a week to set up. First measured-move short after the break is aggressively front-run.
5. Series of measured-move shorts complete their own targets → larger downtrend. About every 10 years, and the busts keep getting bigger.

## The bottoms are TARGETS, not stimulus
- 2008 lows were NOT QE1 / Operation Twist / the 50bp cut — they were a **target** hit, after which a series of measured moves broke. QE1 (Nov 2008) didn't break the short trend; price just rallied into the next measured-move short.
- Every "champagne-cork" euphoric squeeze into a 50% short has the same sentiment: the most adamant, aggressive buyers sit right at the brand-new entry to the short — they're the fuel.

## News doesn't matter — price does
- 2020: the emergency 50bp rate cut (10:00, Tue Mar 3) was SOLD into — because the setup was a short. The two worst jobless-claims prints in history came while price sat inside BULLISH setups → still bullish. "It doesn't matter what the news is."
- Constant across 90+ years: tune out the QEs, the unemployment claims, the arguments — look only at the technical setup.

## The "you are here" tool
- At all-time highs + a broken extension = risk of a large pullback. Inside a full 50% retracement short = it confirms a downside target of a longer-term trend.
- Whatever the extension's 61.8% line number is, THAT is the number that decides bull-continuation vs. bear-entry. Watch it, and "you know where you're at."
- Perception paradox: when you don't understand how trends change, tops seem to happen fast; once you do, they appear to unfold slowly. (Winston Churchill: "The farther back you look, the farther forward you're likely to see.")
