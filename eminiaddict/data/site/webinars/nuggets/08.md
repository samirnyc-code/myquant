**In short:** How to trade the Euro FX futures with ONE contract and small stops: trade micro measured-moves on a 233-tick chart in the direction of the already-identified 15-minute trend — limit orders at 50% retracements only, −12 pip stop, targets at −23.6%, trail 61.8% lines. Never pick tops/bottoms.

## Why the Euro at all
- The currencies trend hard every year (2008-style moves are routine there); Euro ADR ≈ 190 pips and 100 pips ≈ 25 ES points — a range the ES almost never gives you, every day.
- Most liquid currency pair in the world, "extremely technical" — it respects the fib setups; 6B and spot forex do NOT get this treatment (6B less technical on micros; spot's spread forces wider stops and kills it).

## The two strategies
- Standard 15-minute strategy (2 contracts): −16 pip stop, +6 first target, stop moves to −6 after target 1. Full stop ≈ $400 → needs a $40k account at 1% risk.
- Micro strategy (1 contract): −12 pip stop (tighter "will just bite you"), at +6 move stop to −6, at +12 go breakeven. Full stop $150 = 1% on $15k. Start every order with −12 stop / +12 target pre-set.
- Risk reduction inside a valid trend: move the stop to the 61.8% line +1 pip (never against the trade, never more than 12 pips from entry).

## Execution rules (die-hard)
- LIMIT ORDERS ONLY at 50% retracements, front-run by 2 pips on micros (3 fills more reliably; never more than 4). Missed fill = no trade, NO chasing.
- This is NOT for picking turning points or shorting 15-minute 50s — enter micro MMs WITH the 15-minute trend, between known S/R levels, only when there's participation (not chop).
- Know the weekly, daily and 15-minute trend before any trade — "if you don't know what the trend is, don't trade it."
- Take profit at the −23.6% target (most are ~20 pips ≈ $240/contract). Extensions only when a −23.6% target is BLOWN OUT (order: traditionals → extensions → straight down/up).
- Trend change = the 61.8% line break; after it breaks, immediately draw the next opposing 50% and trade the first setup after the break.
- Two failed 61.8% lines (one each way) = a wedge/consolidation EVERY time → no-trade zone until breakout; identify chop, don't trade it.
- Limit exposure: max ~8–10 trades/day; conservative version = only the first 3 micros of a series; stay out around big news (ECB etc.).

## Timing
- Best window: the hour before the European open through ~11:00 ET (European open "is the beginning of the day"); the most technical hour is before the NYSE bell.
- Be careful after Europe closes (noon ET) — liquidity falls off; afternoon failed 61.8s = no trade.
