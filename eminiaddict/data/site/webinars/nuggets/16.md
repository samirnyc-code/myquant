**In short:** "Groundhog Day" — the ES replays its own history with startling date precision, so the past is a roadmap to future targets days/weeks/months out. Beyond traditionals and extensions there are META-trends (a trend OF failures, a trend of blown-past targets, a seasonal trend) and the rule is the same for all of them: **don't fight the trend, whatever KIND of trend it is.** Know your instrument's entire history — every crash, every seasonal, every date.

## The market rhymes — with uncanny date accuracy
- The 2000 top and 2008 top were the SAME pattern: all-the-way-halfway-back short traded ~May 12–14 in both. Both bottomed off a TARGET (not off QE) on ~March 9–10 (2003 and 2009). "Six/eight years later, same pattern, same targets."
- Use it as a blueprint: knowing the replay tells you the long setups of 2012 have targets 200+ points up at 1551 — you can build a forward game-plan and know where to protect vs. profit.

## Meta-trends: there are trends of trends, and you don't fight those either
- **Repeated failures inside a range** (summer 2010 = 2011): first measured move holds to target, the SECOND fails — every time. Trade the range extremes, not the halfway-backs, until it breaks.
- **Blowing past targets without respecting the new extension:** price hits −23.6%, blows through, but the highs-to-highs extension does NOT hold — it runs back to the same anchor/new-high. Once you see the FIRST extension fail this way, don't buy extensions at highs; keep redrawing same-anchor/new-high until the 61.8% finally fails.
- **The extension rule is observational:** you must watch the very first extension. If it holds → trust every extension after it (buy highs). If it fails once → it will fail again; that's a tradeable trend by itself.

## Seasonals worth knowing (his examples)
- **Gold:** halfway-back-for-the-year long in July → rips into year-end. Four years running (2009–2012).
- **Summer doldrums** (late July–early Aug): range-bound, unreliable setups — dangerous to trade.
- **"Sell in May":** 2010/2011/2012 highs all landed ~May 1. **July 4th** rallies/shakeouts.

## The point
- Computers trade a trend until it fails, even against the major trend — that's our edge: we can objectively recognize when a setup that obeys the rules has historically failed at THIS spot, and stand aside or fade it. "Study your charts. There's nothing new under the sun. Go with the flow."
