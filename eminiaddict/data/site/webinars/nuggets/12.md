**In short:** Trade the gap fill PRE-MARKET (8:00–9:30am ET), never at the open. A fill is just a support level below the gap (or resistance above it) that a series of measured moves runs into toward the prior 4pm close. The percentages (64% fill Wednesdays, etc.) are useless — they tell you nothing about TODAY. The real signal is structural: as long as the pre-market series of measured moves toward the fill holds, the gap fills; the moment it breaks its 61.8% line, the gap is dead ("professional gap") and you flip to trade the squeeze.

## Why pre-market, not the open
- The open (9:30) and the close are the two most volatile times — entering a fill there needs a huge stop for a 1:1 payoff. "Entering a gap fill when the market opens is suicide, just send me a check."
- Pre-market is extremely technical / high-participation: you can get a 4-point fill with a 6-tick stop. Same ES tick math as the day method (front-run +2, 1st target +6, stop −6→−4).
- **Be in a FREE trade before any 8:30 news print.** Know when the news is; enter before it so the position is already de-risked. Otherwise it's the same as gambling at the open.

## The setup: only four scenarios, two trends
- Uptrend: **gap DOWN = possible trend change** (counter-trend, buyers fill then sell → can flip the whole trend); **gap UP = continuation** (may skip if already swing-long or making new highs → professional gap risk).
- Downtrend: **gap UP = possible trend change**; **gap DOWN = continuation**.
- You already know the fill entry at yesterday's close — the prior day's last 50%-retracement level IS tomorrow's pre-market entry. "You know it 12 hours before it happens."

## The one rule that overrides the statistics
- **First rule: no clear support/resistance level or series of measured moves toward the gap = NO TRADE.** Sit on your hands.
- Only two exits: target hit (front-run the 4pm close by 2 ticks) OR the trend fails at the 61.8% line. No other way out.
- **Professional gap (won't fill):** if the pre-market series breaks its 61.8% line — or you watch the shorts who sold the fill get blown out / take profit before the open — the gap is over. Don't chase; wait for the 10:00 pullback to the new 50% and trade WITH the gap direction to squeeze the trapped fillers.

## Days to avoid entirely
- Options-expiration Fridays, futures rollover Thursday + the day after, first trading day of a new month/quarter (notorious gap-and-go), and after a narrow-range day when the next gap exceeds the prior day's range.
- Internals confirm, they don't lead: if bank rips / breadth blows out 6:1+ against the fill, raise your alert for a 61.8% break inside the series.

## Bonus — extensions (came up in Q&A)
- Progression is always **traditional → extension → straight up (or down)**. Extensions are drawn highs-to-highs (mirror lows-to-lows), only AFTER a −23.6% target is blown through and price is closer to the −61.8% line than the −23.6%.
- You can never identify an extension in advance — trade at the anchor first, see reaction, then draw. If confused, trade ONLY traditionals; you miss entries but stops stay safe.
- Fail an extension → don't hunt a new 50; expect a full halfway-back and let it come. "Make yourself mechanical even when it doesn't feel good — it's always better than my decision-making."
