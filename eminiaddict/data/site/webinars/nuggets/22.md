**In short:** Three ways to take profit inside a measured move, so a large or slow setup pays you in bites instead of all-or-nothing at the −23.6% target. **Distance formula** = scalp/first-target (fastest). **Trailing the series** = ride traditionals→extensions to the full target, out when the 61.8% fails. **Confirmation** = take partial when an OPPOSING measured move breaks. The single distinction that governs everything: series vs. confirmation is *whether the 61.8% line broke.*

## Take profit on the timeframe you ENTERED on
- Every setup has its own target: micro→micro target, 15-min→15-min, daily→daily. You can't promote a small trade into a swing trade.
- Only exception: the VERY FIRST smaller-timeframe measured move inside a trend can be held to the larger target (e.g. first 15-min long → daily target). The 2nd/3rd/4th setups each go to their OWN target.

## 1) Distance formula — the scalp / first target
- Distance from 50% to 61.8% line = distance from 50% to 38.2% line. So just use the **38.2% line as the first target** (front-run +2 ES / +4 euro entry, exit 38.2, done). Don't do the math in the heat of the moment — draw the 38 light and use it.
- Programs set their first targets this way — enter at 50, bank at 38, then their stop swings out to the 61.8%. That's why the pattern repeats.
- Best reserved for the **summer doldrums / chop**, not trending markets (trends have big targets — scalping wastes them). Use it ONCE per setup, on the first test. Bonus: banking the bigger first target (e.g. +12/+20) lets you tuck a tighter stop under the lows and still pocket a few even if "stopped out of a free trade."

## 2) Trailing the series — stay in the big trend
- Enter, trail the 61.8% line of each retracement as the series goes traditional → (blows through target) → extension → straight up/down. Each new failure level is your line in the sand.
- **Series breaks = 61.8% line breaks → take profit, it's over.** Most series die right after an opposing measured move is broken.

## 3) Confirmation — partial profit + re-entry
- When price breaks the 61.8% of an OPPOSING measured move, your target is CONFIRMED — and you also now know price will come **all the way halfway back**. "Made 40 pips? First thought: I'm about to give back 20 in a pullback."
- Take partial (or all) at that break; the pullback is the next measured move — an entry/add to the larger target, with an average fill below the 61.8%.
- **The ONLY difference between series and confirmation is whether the 61.8% broke.** If the long defended (no break), you're just trailing the series down; you do NOT sell the halfway-back short — you only get the opposing short if the long actually FAILED.

## Use the −23.6% target straight (no bites) when…
- The setup is too small for a series (a micro), OR the position matters more than intraday P&L (a swing/hedge on a daily/weekly level), OR it's the first move of a larger series held to the big target. Weekly setups have the highest probability of hitting targets — always be thinking about how to get a swing on, and how to hedge one you have (e.g. with ES).
- Break-trend / take-profit trigger = **one tick / one pip** past the line. That's all you need.
