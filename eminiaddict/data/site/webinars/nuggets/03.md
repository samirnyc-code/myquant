**In short:** A self-teaching drill that trades the overnight/RTH gap fill with stacked limit orders at the 10/20 handles — but ONLY on days where BOTH the day-of-week stats AND the opening distance say there's an edge.

## Deciding IF the drill is on (the edge filter)
- Check the site's gap-fill statistics over 30 / 90 / 180 days, per weekday — the three windows show the RATE OF CHANGE, not just the average (his example: Mondays 66% → 61% → 50% = decaying, no edge; Wednesdays 59% → 74% → 86% = improving edge).
- Opening distance from yesterday's 16:00 close: inside 10 handles = 91% full-gap fill; 10–20 handles = 76%; more than 20 handles = only 43% — "that's not an edge."
- A pro gap-and-go (big gap, >20 handles) on a weak weekday = do not trust the day; skip the drill.

## The drill itself
- Night before a day with an edge: stack limit orders to BUY at 10 and 20 handles below the prior close, and to SELL at 10 and 20 above.
- Filled at both levels → your average fill is the midpoint (e.g. fills at 53 and 43 → average 48); the stop goes beyond the 30 handle.
- Size so the worst case (full stop-out from the average fill to the 30 handle) never exceeds the 1% risk-per-trade rule.
- Target = the gap fill with a FRONT-RUN OF FOUR ticks (not the usual two — there's competition to get out at the fill; he came within 3 ticks that morning and a 2-tick front-run would have been left behind).
- Gap fills overnight (Asian/European session)? Trade over — RESTACK the orders and go again; keep restacking until the gap fills during RTH.
- After the bell: half the position exits at a front-run of the HALF-gap fill, the rest at the full gap fill front-run. Once the gap fills during trading hours, it's over.

## Why do it
- The lesson of the drill: gaps very often fill in the OVERNIGHT timeframe — you see it yourself by running the drill, which is the point.
