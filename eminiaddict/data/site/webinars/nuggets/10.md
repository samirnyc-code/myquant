## Market basics
- Trade the four majors vs the dollar (EUR = "fiber", GBP = "cable", JPY, AUD) — liquidity and participation; EUR/USD is the most liquid pair and the most TECHNICAL chart (not the biggest mover — the best combination).
- Best times: the hour before and after the London open and the New York open — that's when programs execute the day's plan; you ride the coattails. Avoid the dead zone (after NY close into the Asian session) — no participation, just chop.

## The fractal, again (his clearest statement of it)
- Domino effect: price changes on the SMALL timeframes first. Micros fail → the 15-minute 50% can fail → the daily trend is in jeopardy. Watch micros only to time the 15-minute failure.
- When ANY 61.8% line breaks, everything FLIPS — programs flip, the next opposing 50% gets sold/bought immediately; draw it at once and wait for price to come to it.
- Never trade against the larger trend; always take trades in the direction of the daily. No new swing shorts until the next measured move has traded.
- Three ways into a trend: (1) blind limit at the 50% (valid — it's the most participated level), (2) the micro break, (3) after micros fail, the next 15-minute measured move. Sometimes the level trades once and never gives another shot.

## Swing-trade position rules (the add rule)
- NEVER add to a swing position if the resulting AVERAGE FILL would sit beyond the 61.8% line — because if the market ever comes halfway back (and it always can), the add takes you out of the whole swing. The full 50% retracement is the only "perfect add."
- Always know your average fill and re-do the math before any add. You don't have to add at all.
- Once price moves away from your 50% entry, trail the stop to the top (bottom) of the 15-minute bar ±1–2 pips — if it trades back to the level, everyone bails; don't wait 10 pips to find out.

## Two trades a day (the exercise)
- Challenge given to a struggling trader: Euro only, European open only, max 2 trades/day (10/week) — two bullets, miss them and you're done for the day.
- Goals: you plan trades better, you wait for the setup, you hold until the trend FAILS, and you learn the power of the trend. ("You will battle over-trading for the rest of your trading career.")

## Spot vs futures
- Same price action; spot = $10/pip vs $12.50, ~4-pip spread vs 1–2, no commissions but you pay in the spread; less efficient, not for scalping, higher tax rate.
- Use futures for the short-term/income trades; use spot (or a split account) to carry the multi-month wealth-creation swings — the 100+ pip daily-trend moves (often ~10:1 reward/risk at 1–1.5% risk) and the multi-thousand-pip yearly trends.
- Set price ALERTS instead of watching the screen all day — be 110% present when levels are in play, rest when nothing is happening.
