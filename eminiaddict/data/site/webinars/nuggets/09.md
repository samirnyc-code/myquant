**In short:** The fractal trend-change model: daily trends fail when 15-minute setups fail, 15-minute trends fail when MICROS fail. The 61.8% break is always the first clue; after any break, immediately draw the next opposing measured move — the market ALWAYS eventually comes halfway back.

## The three-timeframe cascade
- Top-down first (weekly → daily → 15-min → micro), then bottom-up: micros are what break everything — "micros fail → the 15-minute can fail → the daily trend is in jeopardy."
- Every time a trend hits its −23.6% target (or a 61.8% line), a counter-trend trade becomes possible. A target being HIT on some timeframe is WHY markets retrace.
- The moment price merely TOUCHES a −23.6% target, draw the next opposing measured move — you don't wait for the trend to visibly change. But the hard rule is the reverse too: you canNOT draw the next measured move until the current −23.6% target has been hit.

## Trend progression and extensions
- Order in a trend: traditionals → extensions → straight down (or up). Traditionals = in-trend; extensions = a bull/bear FLAG; a failed flag goes right back to its 50%.
- Extensions can NEVER be identified ahead of time — "extensions are like a black swan." Price trades at the anchor first; only after you SEE reaction (once, twice, three times…) do you have an anchor, then keep drawing anchor-to-new-lows until it fails. Only traditionals are knowable in advance.
- Safe simplification he endorses: if extensions/anchors confuse you, trade ONLY traditionals — stops stay safe, you just miss some entries. His main use of extensions: locking in profits after −23.6% targets hit.
- "Programs" (algos) attach to these levels on every timeframe — the proof is in the price; a valid 50% will be defended and will NOT trade through.

## Trading rules that came out of it
- After a 61.8% break, don't chase — casually wait for the pullback to the new 50%; it always comes ("the market will always come halfway back after a trend break").
- Resistance rule: FADE the first touch of a level, go WITH the second (that's why double tops/bottoms produce violent moves — trapped traders bail at once).
- TWO failed measured moves (one long, one short) on ANY timeframe = a range/wedge, every time. Halfway backs are no longer sold; trade the range extremes (front-run touches by ~4, more on the Euro) until it breaks, and keep range trades mentally separate.
- Trade the trade you're in: a micro is managed as a micro (out when the micro fails), a 15-minute as a 15-minute. Never promote an intraday trade to a swing or manage a big trade on a tiny timeframe.
- Entry to target beats trailing — "greed kills the trade." Fewer exposures beats more trades: hold trend trades rather than re-entering every leg.
- Have an opinion on every tick or don't trade: know where you are on daily, 15-minute AND micro, at all times.
- His fibs are drawn "backwards" and the 50% isn't a real Fibonacci number — it's man-made "halfway back"; the levels that matter: 50% entries, 61.8% failure, −23.6% targets.
