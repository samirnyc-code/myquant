**In short:** Use the **big timeframe as a road map, drill down to the small one for entries.** Trade in the direction of the daily/weekly target; the first small-timeframe pullback after a trend break is the best entry into the bigger trend.

## Timeframe hierarchy (biggest = most powerful)
- **Weekly** — strongest, highest odds; targets take 3–9 months. Enter it via the **first daily 50%**.
- **Daily** — 2nd; targets 10 days–3 months. Enter it via the **first 15-min 50%**.
- **15-min** — where you spend most time (intraday swing). Enter it via the **first micro** measured move.
- **Micro** — weakest; just a *tool* to time entries into the 15-min. Gets less reliable the further from the setup.
- Price is the same on every timeframe (no "wrong" timeframe); only the setups inside differ.

## Rules of the road
- **Biggest trend usually wins** (more eyes, volume, momentum).
- When a trend **breaks, it trades its full pullback** — a weekly break makes a weekly opposing move, a daily break a daily move, etc.
- A target is **confirmed** when the smaller trend inside breaks its **opposing measured move** — that same break sets up the next entry.
- **All roads lead to Rome:** know the daily/weekly target (path of least resistance) → wait for a 15-min setup in that direction → use the micro to enter with great R:R.

## Entry progression (same as the entries lesson)
- First test → front-run 2nd test → trend-break + next measured move. If a leg fights a larger setup, wait for the trend break.

## Instrument filter
- Trade the technical markets: ES/indices (90% institutional), the FX majors (EUR, CAD, AUD, GBP; JPY too). **If a market doesn't respect its largest timeframe, trade a different instrument.**
