**In short:** Price/trend is the default; **Bank, Breadth, TICK, Time & Sales** are *leading* tools that confirm or oppose it. The more tools agreeing, the more reliable the trend. "Make the case to the jury."

## Leading vs lagging
- We use **leading** tools only (predict what's next). Anything off a moving average is **lagging** = noise.

## Bank (Nasdaq Banking Index, 15-min)
- Financials are heavily weighted in the S&P and move in exaggerated fashion → early clue.
- Bank new highs & ES not → bank can **drag ES up** (mirror for lows). ES new highs **without** bank → don't trust the rally (bull trap); mirror = bear trap. **Don't fight bank** — but the trend is valid first.

## Breadth (up-vol vs down-vol, advances vs decliners)
- Positive = uptrending, negative = downtrending, mixed = likely sideways.
- **Know where breadth OPENED.** −30→−12 by midday is a *strengthening* (bullish-turning) market even though still negative. If you didn't see the open, don't trade. Breadth **confirms the day's direction**.

## TICK (1-min, with a 1-period SMA)
- Shows brief overbought/oversold. **High tick = selling op, low tick = buying op** (proactive, not reactive). "If you see it and you're not in it, it's over."
- **±400** is the line — buy only in a **≤ −400** low tick, sell only in a **≥ +400** high tick → times your reduced-risk/free trades.
- **Tick hook:** the SMA rolling over from a high tick into a low (or vice versa) *at your pre-identified 50% level* = the green light to enter with best odds of the first target.

## TICK extremes & divergences (see the dedicated lesson)
- High/low tick matching price = **profit-taking signal, NOT an automatic reversal.** It's a **divergence** (trend continues, hold) if price makes a new extreme without a new tick; it's a **reversal** only if the trend then breaks + confirms. The old "sell any +1000 tick" rule is dangerous.

## Time & Sales
- The tape = participation. Seeing size trade off your 50% (on the down-tick for a short) **confirms programs are on that level** → validates the target.

## Putting it together
- **Daily trend/target = default direction.** Tools confirm or oppose. Long trend + bullish divergence + bank up + positive/ rising breadth + T&S support at each 50% = strong case. Mixed tools = choppy; stand aside.
- Tools matter far more in high-participation season (Sept→May): 5–7 ES setups/day vs 2–3 all week in summer.
