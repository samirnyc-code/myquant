**In short:** Markets repeat seasonally. Bias is **risk-on / protect-profits** (most money is long-only). Trade the fast months, the mid-week, and the two daily volume pushes (~10:00 ET and ~3:00 ET Europe open); avoid the slow windows.

## Months (the year's map)
- **Jan–Mar:** bullish, fast. **Apr–May:** best months to **sell / take profit** ("sell in May"). May = worst month to buy.
- **Jun–Jul–Aug:** consolidation, slow (managers "on vacation" Memorial Day→Labor Day); wide ranges but choppy — worst time to trade indices. Aug = worst month to *sell*.
- **Sep:** best time to **buy**; **Sep–Dec** fast again (early "Santa" accumulation). New positions really go on at **start of Q4 into May**, not January.

## Days of the week
- **Mon:** slow (digesting news, planning). **Tue–Thu:** the "heavy lifting" — fast, high volume, risk-on, positions go on. **Fri:** dead after the Europe close (profit-take/hedge, risk-off).
- Intraday volume shape most days: high open → dip midday → **ramp into the close**.

## Hours (ET) — when to act
- **2:00** German open (observe bias); **3:00 London open** = often an *exact reversal* of the German open — one of the two best 15-min windows (3:00–3:15).
- **5:30–7:30** Europe doldrums (lunch) — avoid micros. **8:00–8:30** = entry into the gap fill (90 min before the bell).
- **9:30–10:00 = NO-TRADE** (reactionary, whippy, full stop-outs). **10:00–10:15 = the single best entry window** of the US day; **10:00–11:30** fast/risk-on.
- **11:30** reversal period → take partial profit regardless of target (everyone hedges into lunch). **11:30–1:30 doldrums** = 15-min setups only. **2:00–close** moderate; **3:00–3:45 last push** toward the next day's daily pivot.

## Worst times (don't trade)
- **6:30pm–2:00am** Asian session (ES/FX slow & choppy; AUD/gold can trend). Europe & US **doldrums** = 15-min trends only. **9:30–10:00** open.
- Doldrums options: trade a 15-min setup at its exact entry, **sit on your hands**, or manage an existing position. Nothing else. "Be the ball" — match the market's speed.
