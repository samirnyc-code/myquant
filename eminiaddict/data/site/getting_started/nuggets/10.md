**TL;DR:** There are only three setups — the **50% retracement**, the **61.8% failure** (a signal, not a limit), and the **extension**. Trade them on **15-min charts**, hold to the **123% (−23.6%) target**, risk ≤1%.

## The three setups
- **Traditional 50%:** after a rally/sell-off price pulls halfway back; buy/sell the 50%, target 123%. Chain them into a **series** (target → pullback → next 50%). A series is "live" once two have completed.
- **61.8% failure:** not something you place — it's a **signal**. When the opposing measured move breaks its 61.8% line, buy the **first pullback** after the trend break. Highest-participation entry; can ride to the larger-timeframe target.
- **Extension:** price blows **past** a 123% target without pulling back. Redraw the 50% from the **last low → new highs** (mirror for shorts). Looks like a traditional — just a different anchor.

## Series & the anchor
- A traditional series ends two ways: it **breaks a 61.8% line** (→ trend change) or **blows out of target** (→ starts extensions).
- Extensions **reuse the same anchor to new highs** while they keep hitting target. Only when one **blows past target** do you start a **new** extension from the last high (extension-of-extension) — that's when the anchor advances.
- Extensions that just **stall at target** can give back ~90% of the move; the big runs come from expansions past target ("extension to extension to extension").

## Which NOT to trust (no limit — watch for participation)
- The **first extension off a brand-new anchor** — observe only. No participation → it fails, and the whole trend fails (stop goes just under the extension).
- A next measured move **after a blown-out target** (the extension already failed).
- A setup running **into a larger-timeframe target or S/R** — the bigger timeframe wins.
- A 61.8% break that **retests the prior high/low (double top/bottom)** before pulling back — "whatever trades first"; skip it.

## When / how much
- **15-min charts** (medium timeframe, not micro). Bigger setup = more reliable.
- Sessions: Euro 3:00–11:30 ET; ES 8:00–11:30 & 13:30–close ET (skip 9:30–10:00). Risk **1%**; let the market's range set the target size.
