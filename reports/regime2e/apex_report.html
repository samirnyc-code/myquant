⚠ Apex is NOT the Take Profit Trader account we modeled. Apex's drawdown trails your peak — it is a moving threshold, not a static floor at start−$4,500. This changes the survival math and it is the single most important thing to get right.

How the drawdown works (both models)

The trailing threshold follows your highest balance minus the threshold amount, then locks permanently at start + $100 once your balance reaches the Safety Net = start + threshold + $100. Pre-safety-net you may use only half the max contracts.
  • EOD model — trails your highest end-of-day balance; recalculated 4:59 PM ET. Has a daily loss limit. Workable for a hold-to-EOD book.
  • Intraday model — trails the real-time peak including unrealized open profit, every tick. "Daily Drawdown: None" in your screenshot ⇒ the legacy FULL accounts are this one. Bad fit: our winners take heat and give back intraday, which ratchets the floor against us.

Rules that bite a lumpy book

  • 50% consistency rule (funded accounts only, not eval): no single day may be >50% of total profit since your last payout. Our book is fat-tailed — one big trend day (e.g. a +$10k FOMC day) can exceed 50% and defer a payout. Mitigate: request payouts often, spread profit across days first.
  • Payouts: min $500; 8 trading days between payouts; 6 payouts then the account closes; 100% of first $25k, then 90/10.
  • Flat + orders cancelled by 4:59 PM ET (we flatten at the RTH close — fine). No overnight. No news hedging.
  • Eval: profit goal above, ~5–7 qualifying days, no consistency rule.

Recommendation

1. Confirm the account is EOD, not intraday. If only intraday FULL is offered, this book is a poor fit there.
2. On EOD, 150K + one-per-day is the value pick — smallest eval goal ($9k, fastest to pass at low size), survives with ~$1.2k ramp margin, cheapest at $49.70/mo.
3. Prefer 250K if the thin backtest margin worries you (it should — backtests flatter): ~$1,500 more ramp cushion for +$10/mo, at the cost of a $15k eval.
4. Pass the eval faster by sizing up during the eval only (cap allows 8/13/17), then drop to 1 ES on the funded account.
5. flip mode on 150K clears the floor by only $235 — don't; use one-per-day, or a bigger plan for flip.
