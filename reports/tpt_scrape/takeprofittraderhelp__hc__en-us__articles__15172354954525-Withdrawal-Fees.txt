Withdrawal Fees
 Vlada
8 months ago Updated

Take Profit Trader waives all fees on withdrawals from the wallet over $250. 

If you request a withdrawal of $250 or less from your wallet, you will be charged a $50 withdrawal fee. This fee will be deducted automatically when you make your request.

If you select PayPal as the payout option, additional fees might get applied to the withdrawals by PayPal. These will be deducted from the withdrawal amounts.
