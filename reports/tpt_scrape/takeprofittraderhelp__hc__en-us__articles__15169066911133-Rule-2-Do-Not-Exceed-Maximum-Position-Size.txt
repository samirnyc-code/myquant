Maximum Position by Account Size

Your maximum position size is a static number of contracts that you can have open at once, depending on what size account you choose to trade. You cannot exceed this limit for your account.  

Futures Account Size	Maximum Position Size
$25,000 	3 Contracts / 30 Micros
$50,000 	6 Contracts / 60 Micros
$75,000	9 Contracts / 90 Micros
$100,000	12 Contracts / 120 Micros
$150,000	15 Contracts / 150 Micros

Please note that you are allowed 10x position size on permitted Micro products. For example on the $50K account you can trade up to 60 micro contracts.
