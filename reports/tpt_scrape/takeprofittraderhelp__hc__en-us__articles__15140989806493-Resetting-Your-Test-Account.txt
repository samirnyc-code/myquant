What is an Account Reset?  

An account reset gives you a second chance to start over with your trading test. It’s typically used when your initial test didn’t go as planned — for example, if you broke a rule or want a clean slate to try again.

 

How Can I Reset My Account?
Step 1: Access Your Dashboard

Go to takeprofittrader.com.

Log in to your account using your registered email and password.

Navigate to the Subscriptions section of your dashboard.

 

Step 2: Reset Your Account

You can reset your account in two ways:

From the Subscriptions page:
Click the Reset Account button next to the subscription you wish to restart.

From the Account Information window:
Open your account details and select Reset Account from the control options.

After confirming, you’ll receive a new trading test with the same parameters and rules as your original test.




 

Important Note

Please note that this will not change the subscription renewal date — it will only give you a fresh account to work with.
