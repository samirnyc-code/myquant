How to Check Your Renewal Date
 Vlada
8 months ago Updated

You can check your subscription renewal date by logging into your dashboard and selecting the Subscriptions section.

 

Monthly Renewal Time

Your subscription renews at the same time of day each month.
For example, if you started your trading test at 10:00 AM, your subscription will renew on the renewal date at 10:00 AM.


 

Reset Policy on Renewal

TakeProfitTrader resets trading accounts during monthly renewal only if the account is disabled.

If your account is active, the monthly subscription renewal will not trigger a reset.
