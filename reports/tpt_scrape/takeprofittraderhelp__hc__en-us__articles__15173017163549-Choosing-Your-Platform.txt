Choosing Your Platform
 Vlada
9 months ago Updated

Each trader chooses their platform when signing up at Take Profit Trader. We provide connection instructions for the platforms inside the Connection section of this knowledge base.

For most platforms you will need a license key, but there are some offered for free. 

Depending on the data feed you select, you can seamlessly switch between compatible platforms during your subscription period. Simply follow the connection instructions provided for the new platform, there is no need to inform us.


Here is a list of available platforms:
 
Platform	Free During Test (Yes/No)	Web/Mobile Based	Data Feed
TradingView	Yes*	Yes	CQG
NinjaTrader	Yes	Mobile	CQG
Tradovate	Yes	Yes	CQG
Bookmap	No	No	Rithmic
MultiCharts	No	No	Rithmic
MotiveWave	No	No	Rithmic
Trade Navigator	No	No	Rithmic
R|Trader	Yes	Mobile	Rithmic
VolFix	No	No	Rithmic
ATAS	No	No	Rithmic
Investor R/T	No	No	Rithmic
Quantower	Yes	No	Rithmic
Finamark	No	No	Rithmic
Jigsaw Trader	No	No	Rithmic
*You can use ''Basic'' plan on TradingView for free. However, if you are on the 'Basic' plan, be aware that there may be slight delays in the data, as this plan does not include the fastest data flow feature. As a result, you may experience small discrepancies in the prices, which can result in worse filled prices than expected. Keep in mind that Take Profit does not have a direct affiliation with TradingView and cannot be held responsible for these discrepancies.
