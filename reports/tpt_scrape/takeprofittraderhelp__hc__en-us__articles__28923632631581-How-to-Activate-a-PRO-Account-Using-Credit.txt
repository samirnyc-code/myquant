How to Activate a PRO Account Using Credit
 Vlada
8 months ago Updated

Some promotional codes include a benefit that waives the standard $130 activation fee for a PRO account, by providing you with a PRO Activation credit instead. 

Note: Even when a promo code covers the activation fee, you must still manually complete the activation steps in your dashboard.

Important: 
If a test account is passed on a Friday trading day, the activation credit will be issued on the next business day (Monday) as part of our standard processing schedule. Activation credits are not issued over the weekend.

1. Start by signing in to your Take Profit Trader account. Once inside, head over to the PRO section. Click the Go to PRO button.

2. When asked to choose a payment method, select Your credits. The credit will be automatically added to your dashboard once you successfully pass the test and your PRO account is approved.

That’s it! At this point, you may proceed with signing the contract and POA form, if you haven’t done so yet.
