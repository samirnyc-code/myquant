Approved Products

You may trade most futures products listed on:

CME

CBOT

NYMEX

COMEX

If an order is rejected for a product you believe should be allowed, contact support.
You can review the full list of permitted products here.

 

Approved Trading Hours

Positions cannot be carried from one trading day into the next.
All positions must be opened and closed within the approved daily window:

Futures:

Trading window: 6 PM Eastern – 5 PM Eastern

The trading day ends at 5 PM Eastern

TPT will automatically close any open position at 4:55 PM Eastern.

You may reopen positions starting at 6 PM Eastern, but any trade opened at or after 6 PM counts toward the next trading day.

 

Be Aware of Market Hours

Always know the specific hours for the product you’re trading.
If the market for your product closes before 5 PM Eastern and you cannot exit before the deadline, the account will be liquidated.

 

Holiday Schedule

Market hours can change due to holidays.
It is the trader’s responsibility to monitor holiday schedules.
Missing a holiday-adjusted close time will result in account liquidation.
