What is a PRO+ Account?

PRO+ accounts are live accounts that have a $0 margin. Upon transitioning to a PRO+ account, the trader is classified as a professional trader. 

How Do I Become Eligible for a PRO+ Account? 

Once a trader becomes eligible for a PRO+ upgrade, an official invitation will be sent via email by the Take Profit Trader team. Please note that the decision to upgrade a trader to a PRO+ account is made solely at the discretion of Take Profit Trader.

Benefits of a PRO+ Account
90/10 profit split, where the trader keeps 90% of the profits
Trader directly routed to the exchange, not on the simulation or ''copy traded''
No buffer zone requirement for withdrawal
EOD (End-Of-Day) Drawdown
