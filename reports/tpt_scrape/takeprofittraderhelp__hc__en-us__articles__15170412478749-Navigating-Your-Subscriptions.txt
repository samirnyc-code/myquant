What a Subscription Means

A subscription is your pathway toward earning a specific PRO account.
Under one subscription, you can make multiple attempts (resets) to reach that PRO account without creating a new user.

Your Take Profit Trader login gives you one user profile.
Inside that profile, you can have:

multiple subscriptions

multiple trading accounts

multiple attempts under each subscription

Each subscription represents its own “Journey to Profit.”

 

Viewing Your Subscriptions

When you log in, you will see all your subscriptions in one list.
Each subscription displays:

subscription status (active or disabled)

renewal date

trading accounts created under that subscription

Subscriptions renew every 30 days until canceled.


 

Access After Cancellation

If you cancel a subscription, you still keep full access for the remainder of the billing cycle.
This ensures that you always receive the full 30 days you paid for.
 

Opening a Subscription Dashboard

Clicking on a subscription opens the trading dashboard for that specific journey.
From there, you can view:

your trading accounts

account metrics

platform credentials

progress toward targets

your “How Am I Doing?” section

your NinjaTrader license key

You can switch between subscriptions by using the navigation that lists your “Journey to Profit” paths.



Viewing All Accounts Under a Subscription

Each subscription can contain multiple trading accounts if you choose to reset.
To view all accounts under one subscription:

click the arrow next to the subscription name

all accounts created during past attempts will expand in a list

This allows you to review each attempt individually, check trades, and analyze your performance history.
