What is a PRO Reset? 

A PRO reset gives you the ability to skip the evaluation and have the opportunity to get back to trading your PRO account while still being able to withdraw from day-one. As a convenience to our PRO traders, here at TakeProfitTrader we allow you to reset your PRO Account up to three times per account. An account reset is a fresh account, with all the same properties and rules as your original PRO account. 

How to Reset your PRO Account

Resetting a PRO account is similar to resetting a test account. 

Login to takeprofittrader.com. 

Go to the PRO section of your dashboard.

Find the PRO account and click the 'reset account' button. 

Enter and submit your payment information. 

Cost of a PRO Reset 

25k PRO Reset: $449

50k PRO Reset: $649

75k PRO Reset: $799

100k PRO Reset: $999

150k PRO Reset: $1499

Resetting a PRO account is completely optional. The costs associated are a convenience fee and are not used to post capital or fulfill margin requirements as PRO Accounts exist in a simulated environment.
