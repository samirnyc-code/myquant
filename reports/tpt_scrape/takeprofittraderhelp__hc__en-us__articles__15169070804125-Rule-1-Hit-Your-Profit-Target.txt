Profit Targets by Account Size

Each Take Profit Trader account has its own required profit target.
To see the exact target for your account size, check the table provided for all account types.
 

Futures Account Size	Maximum Contracts	Profit Target
$25,000	3 Contracts	$1,500
$50,000 	6 Contracts	$3,000
$75,000 	9 Contracts	$4,500
$100,000 	12 Contracts	$6,000
$150,000	15 Contracts	$9,000
