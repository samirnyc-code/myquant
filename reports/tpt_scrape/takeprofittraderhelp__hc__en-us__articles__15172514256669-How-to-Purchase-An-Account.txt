How to Purchase An Account
 Vlada
8 months ago Updated
1. Log In and Navigate to Your Dashboard
Once you have registered for an account, please log in at www.takeprofittrader.com using your credentials. 

Inside your dashboard, locate and click the “Create New” button to begin the signup process for an evaluation account. 




2. Choose Your Account Settings

Select your preferred account size. 
 

2. Pick your desired data feed, either Rithmic or CQG, from the available options.
 

3. Apply Promo Code (If Applicable)

On the next screen, you’ll have the opportunity to enter a promotional code (if applicable).

After that, choose your payment method for the test account subscription. 

4. Complete the Payment

Enter your payment information as required.

Submit the form to finalize the purchase of your test account. 
 

Your test account and platform credentials should populate within a few minutes. Follow these steps to finish the activation of your account. 

If your account credentials do not populate within an hour of purchase, please contact our Live Chat Support for assistance.
