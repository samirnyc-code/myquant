Connecting NinjaTrader 8 with Rithmic
 Vlada
2 months ago Updated

In order to connect your Rithmic account to Ninjatrader, please follow these steps. Please make sure to have completed the Rithmic connection steps described in this article before connecting to NinjaTrader for the first time with your Rithmic account.

If you prefer a visual walkthrough, we've included a video guide to help you. For those who prefer a written explanation, you’ll find a detailed step-by-step breakdown just below the video.



 

Connecting Your Rithmic Account to NinjaTrader
1. Creating a Ninjatrader Account (if you're already registered with NinjaTrader, there's no need to register again, you can bypass this section)
Navigate to NinjaTrader's official webpage. Click on Log In and locate the Get Started section.
Enter your email address and click Sign Up
Please check your inbox and confirm your email.
Once your email is confirmed, create your username and password.
Enter a desired username and a strong password. Be sure to store them securely, as you'll need them to access NinjaTrader in the future.
Review the Terms and Conditions and check the box to confirm your agreement.
Complete the registration.
2. Downloading NinjaTrader
If you haven't installed the NinjaTrader application on your computer, you can find the download link in the Additional Options section.
Download and install the NinjaTrader application.
Once complete, open the application.
3. Connecting to Rithmic
To log in, use the credentials you used to create your NinjaTrader account.
When launching NinjaTrader, several pop-up windows with various indicators and charts may appear. To set up your connection, focus specifically on the Control Center window.
Before proceeding, please log into your TakeProfitTrader dashboard to have your Rithmic credentials readily available for the connection setup. To access your Rithmic credentials, please login into your user dashboard using your TakeProfitTrader credentials. Locate your Rithmic subscription within your TakeProfitTrader dashboard to access the necessary credentials for connection.
Return to the NinjaTrader Control Center window.
In the top menu bar, click on the Connections section and select Configure.
In case you don't see a configure option, please go to Tools and select Options. In the General Category section, find MultiProvider under Properties and check the box. Don't forget to click Apply, bend the OK button, and restart your NinjaTrader.
Locate Rithmic for NinjaTrader Brokerage and add it to your connections list.
Enter your Rithmic credentials from the TakeProfitTrader dashboard and paste them into the Connections tab within the NinjaTrader connection settings.
Please select Rithmic Paper Trading and Chicago as the system. Click Apply and OK.
Select your Rithmic for NinjaTrader from the Connections drop-down menu. When the green connection circle appears on the left, you are connected. 

You're now connected and ready to begin your trading journey with us! You can open a new chart window or use the ones that appeared upon launch. Don't forget to choose your TakeProfitTrader account from the chart menu.

Now it's time to dive in and start trading. Best of luck!
