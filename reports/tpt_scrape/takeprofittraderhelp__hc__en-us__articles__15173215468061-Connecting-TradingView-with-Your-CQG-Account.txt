Connecting TradingView with Your CQG Account

6 months ago Updated

Please complete all connection steps described in this article before connecting to TradingView for the first time.

TradingView is a web-based charting and trading platform that is also available as a mobile app for:

iOS 14.0 or later (App Store)

Android 7.0 or later (Google Play)

To access the web platform, click here.

If you prefer a visual walkthrough, follow the steps shown in the instructional video. A written, detailed step-by-step explanation is provided below.
 

Follow these quick steps to connect your TakeProfitTrader CQG account to TradingView and start trading.


1. Create or Log in to TradingView
Go to TradingView.com
Sign up for a new account or log in to your existing one
2. Open Supercharts
From the top navigation menu, click Products → Supercharts
This will open the main TradingView charting workspace
3. Open the Trading Panel
Click the Trade button at the top-right side
In the list of available brokers, find Tradovate
Make sure the Demo environment is selected
Click Connect
4. Get Your Tradovate Credentials
Log in to your TakeProfitTrader Dashboard
Navigate to your CQG / Tradovate account subscription
Copy the Tradovate username and password provided for your account
5. Log in via Tradovate Verification Page
Paste your username and password into the Tradovate login window
Click Login to authorize the connection
6. Connection Confirmed
Once connected, your trading accounts will appear in the Trading Panel below the chart
You can now place trades directly from TradingView
