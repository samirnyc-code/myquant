Desk · Trade Playbook

The fields we fill out for every setup

One rule: we only trade what's written here. Each setup gets defined once — thesis, the exact entry gate, risk, sizing, and annotated screenshots — so a live decision is just a yes/no check against the card. Below is every field on the form, and what goes in it.

Thesis

Why this is +EV. If you can't write the edge in one line, you don't have one.

One-line edgetext
The single reason this makes money.Second failed poke below prior-day low → responsive buyers take it back into value
Full rationalelong text
The auction / order-flow / structural logic. What conditions create the opportunity and why it repeats.
Day type / auction context requiredlong text
The backdrop that has to be present — day type and where price sits.Trend up/down · balance/rotation · open-drive vs open-auction · vs prior-day value, VWAP, IB

Confirmationlong text
