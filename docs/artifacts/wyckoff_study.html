Wyckoff Study — a tradeable ES system

Everything distilled from Villahermosa's two books (The Wyckoff Methodology in Depth and Wyckoff 2.0: Structures, Volume Profile & Order Flow), organised into one reference for trading ES on the 2000-tick and 5-minute charts. This is a discretionary-assist system: the tools surface context, levels and confirmation; you make the call. No more gurus — Wyckoff structure + auction theory (Dalton, via Volume Profile) + price action (Brooks) is a complete toolkit.

discretionary, not automated2000-tick + 5M ES structure → location → triggerpage-cited to both books

The whole system in one sentence: in the direction of the last shakeout and the last HVN, wait for price to reach a value-area edge or structure edge, and enter only when order flow confirms (absorption → initiative / an SOS-SOW bar) — stop beyond the structure at an LVN, target the next HVN's VPOC.

The 3-layer model + the master rules

Wyckoff 2.0 assigns each tool one job. Keeping these lanes separate is what stops the chart from becoming "a 1-minute chart full of labels" (the book's own named failure mode).

Master rules (memorize these):

  • Bias: trade with the last-developed HVN — price above it → longs only; below → shorts only. Flip only when price effectively breaks that zone. (bk2 p.218-220)
  • Dominant event: always favour the direction of the last shakeout (spring → long, upthrust → short). (bk2 p.28-29, 78)
  • Order flow only at zones: never read the footprint chart-wide — only when price has reached a pre-chosen level. (bk2 p.247, 275)
  • One move at a time: propose only the next move; enter with a stop order, never a limit. (bk2 p.303-305)
  • Labels are provisional: a "spring" that produces no follow-through becomes a test. Read dynamics, not labels. (bk1 p.105-106; bk2 p.67)
  • Confluence wins: the best trades stack a structure event + a VP level + VWAP at one price. (bk2 p.296, 320)

Book 1 — the three laws & the five phases

The 3 laws

  • Supply & demand — aggressive (market) orders move price; passive (limit) orders stop it. (p.29-34)
  • Cause & effect — the sideways range builds the cause; the trend is the effect, proportional to time+volume in the range. (p.35-40)
  • Effort vs result — volume=effort, price=result. High effort + small result = absorption/exhaustion (reversal). Read it relatively, never absolute. (p.41-48)

Phases A–E

  • A — stop the trend (PS · Climax · AR · ST). Ends at the ST.
  • B — build the cause (tests at both edges). Should be longer than A & C.
  • C — the test / shakeout (Spring or Upthrust).
  • D — trend within the range (SOS/SOW, then LPS/BUEC).
  • E — trend outside the range.

(p.134-144). Only trade C / D / E.

The trading-range box

The range is a box whose edges are zones, not lines (draw a band; more touches = more confidence). (bk1 p.147-154; bk2 p.40-42)

  • Top edge (Creek) = the Automatic-Rally high, refined by Phase-B highs. Bottom edge (Ice) = the Automatic-Reaction low, refined by Phase-B lows. The opposite edge = the climax extreme. (p.147, 150)
  • Structure edges are, in profile terms, Low Volume Nodes — price didn't want to trade there. (bk2 p.215-216)
  • If Phase B stops respecting the horizontal edges → switch to a sloping box (draw one edge through A/B/C extremes, clone the other; up-slope = strength, down = weakness). (bk1 p.153-154)

Events — spring vs test vs climax (the part we kept getting wrong)

The rule that fixes the mislabeling: the low that creates the range is the Climax (Phase A) — never a spring. A spring is a Phase-C event, defined retroactively: it is a false break of the already-established edge that then causes the breakout to the opposite side. "Anything other than this… is simply a test." (bk1 p.105-106; bk2 p.67, p.258)

The 5 value-area trading principles

  • Range fade — inside a VA, fade the edges; target VPOC then the opposite edge. (p.237)
  • 80% reversion — a failed break that re-enters the VA → ~80% odds of reaching the opposite edge. (p.239, 327)
  • Continuation — fail to enter the VA / rejected at the edge → imbalance away (the break-and-retest). (p.242)
  • Failed reversion — rejected at the VPOC → reversal cancelled; re-assess. (p.244)
  • Shapes: P = accumulation/strength, b = distribution/weakness; bP → up, Pb → down (= Phases A/B). (p.209-228)

Book 2 — Order Flow (the "when") — from our own footprint

We already have the data. FootprintExporter.cs reconstructs the ES footprint (bid/ask volume per price + delta) free from raw ticks, validated exact vs MzPack. NT's Order Flow+ is not needed.
  • Read diagonally — aggressive buys hit the ASK, sells hit the BID; compare BID[L] vs ASK[L+1]. (p.249-251)
  • Imbalance = 200/300/400% diagonal disparity + a min-contracts floor (calibrate to ES). (p.251-252)
  • Turning pattern = Absorption → Initiative:
    • Absorption (potential): high volume, price stops, imbalances against the close (BID at the bottom for a spring low). (p.253-255)
    • Initiative (confirmation = the SOS/SOW bar): aggression in the entry direction, close at the extreme, delta rotation. Initiative is mandatory; absorption optional. (p.255-257, 330-331)
  • Continuation pattern = Control + Test — trend add, then a low-volume retest of the control level. (p.262-267)
  • Delta = ASK−BID vol; divergence = delta sign ≠ bar direction; absorption = big |delta| + no progress. (p.164, 176-177)
Caveats baked into the tools: delta = aggression, not intent (label it that way); dark-pool / CME block trades are hidden (~24h delayed); order flow is meaningless without structural context.

The integrated workflow

1
Context — read the structure (are we range-at-edge, range-inside, trend-breakout, or trend-pullback?). Set bias from the last shakeout + last HVN.
2
Location — pick the VP level to wait at (VAL/VAH, structure edge = LVN, broken Creek/Ice, prior VPOC/VWAP). Look for confluence.
3
Scenario — "if X then Y", with an alternative for the opposite outcome.
4
Trigger & manage — only now open the footprint at that zone: absorption → initiative / SOS-SOW bar. Enter stop-order, stop at LVN, target next HVN→VPOC. Breakeven at first VA target.

(bk2 p.277-278, 300-311)

Applying it on ES — 2000-tick & 5-minute

Volume works on the tick chart — if you measure it right. A 2000-tick bar has ~constant trade count but variable contract volume. Measure effort on swings (Weis wave), not per bar. VP levels are built from contracts-per-price, so they are identical on any bar type. (bk2 p.17-24, 203)
  • 5M = context, 2000t = execution. Read structure + draw the box + mark VP levels on the 5M (and daily for the macro HVN/VWAP); drop to 2000t for the spring/test and the footprint trigger. The book's own rule: top-down for context, bottom-up for the trigger; a 2000t micro-range at a 5M edge is the 5M event (nesting). (bk1 p.206-212)
  • Never use volume-based bars (they disable volume analysis). Tick and time both fine. (bk2 p.22)
  • Segregate RTH vs ETH volume — the US open prints huge volume; don't compare RTH vs ETH magnitudes raw. (Validates our two-trove RTH/ETH split.) (bk2 p.17-19)
  • Effort read on 2000t: Weis-wave (volume per swing), range (spread, undistorted on tick), and — at the trigger bar only — tempo (ticks/sec) as the "loud vs quiet" gauge.

Future indicators / tools to build (staged)

Stage 1 — foundation: levels + effort

  • Volume Profile engine (from the tick troves → drawn in NT8): VPOC, VAH/VAL (68.2%), HVN/LVN, naked/developing VPOC; session / composite / fixed-range scopes.
  • VWAP session + weekly ± 1σ/2σ.
  • Weis-wave (volume per swing) — the tick-safe effort meter.
  • Last-HVN bias line + price-vs-VWAP/VPOC state (the master gate).
  • Footprint layer from FootprintExporter: delta, diagonal imbalance, absorption, initiative — computed but only surfaced at zones.

Stage 2 — structure tools

  • "Wyckoff TR Box" drawing tool — draw the whole range; auto-partition Phases A–E; edges as LVN-snapped zones; sloping mode.
  • Retroactive event engine — climax = range edge; spring/upthrust confirmed only by the opposite-edge breakout; test = No Supply/No Demand; structural failure; shortening-of-thrust.

Stage 3 — trigger + management (the integration)

  • At an armed zone: absorption + initiative + SOS/SOW bar → propose stop-entry, SL at LVN, TP at HVN→VPOC / naked VPOC, R/R + alternative scenario.
  • A single "setup ready" panel: shows the active context, the level, the confluence count, and the missing ingredient ("waiting for initiative").

Design contract: structure builds the scenario, VP sets location, OF times the trigger — OF never originates a trade, never trade against the last-HVN bias, never spray marks chart-wide.

The playbook (draft — to harden over the first 100 trades)

Each trade must pass all four gates. If any is missing, no trade.

GateQuestionPass condition
1 ContextWhich of the 4 setups is this, and does bias agree?Price on the correct side of the last HVN; trading with the last shakeout.
2 LocationIs price at a real level?At a VAL/VAH, structure edge, broken Creek/Ice, or prior VPOC/VWAP — ideally ≥2 stacked (confluence).
3 TriggerDid order flow confirm?Absorption → initiative (SOS/SOW bar with delta rotation) at the zone.
4 RiskIs the trade worth it?Stop at the protecting LVN; nearest HVN→VPOC target gives ≥ 2R; ≤ 1–2% risk; alternative scenario written.

Management: enter on a stop order at the trigger bar; move to breakeven at the first VA target; scale/hold a runner to the next HVN. Exit fully on an ultra-high-volume opposing climax or the opposite structure's Phase A/C. (bk2 p.302-311)

What's testable + the 100-trade refinement loop

  • Testable now (against the trove): value-area reversion (80% rule), fade-the-edge, break-and-retest continuation, and the spring/upthrust-with-confluence edge — all with entry/stop/target rules above. This is exactly the kind of frozen-spec, train/test study we already run on the tempo engine.
  • Not fully testable (discretionary): reading which of the 4 contexts is live, and confluence judgement — these need the forward record.
  • The loop: trade the playbook → journal every trade with its gates + the data (below) → after ~100 trades, measure which setups/levels/triggers actually paid → retune the indicators to your style (e.g., imbalance ratio, "quiet test" threshold, which VP levels to trust). This is the plan you described.

Data recording & the custom journal

Recording every tick + bid/ask + volume requires an always-on recorder during market hours. ES trades ~23h/day (Sun 5pm CT → Fri 4pm CT, with a ~1h daily break) — not literally 24/7, but "on whenever ES trades." A recorder must be connected to the feed the whole time; anything it misses (crash/off) is lost for bid/ask (trade ticks can sometimes backfill, L1 usually can't).
  • For live trading you do NOT need the archive — the indicators compute footprint from the live feed in real time. The 24h recording is for the data asset: backtesting the tools, building historical Volume Profiles, and the journal/refinement loop.
  • Recommended: a dedicated tick recorder (bid/ask/volume → the DuckDB store) with a watchdog, running alongside NT during all ES hours; Databento as a paid backfill safety-net for any gaps.

The custom journal (tied to this playbook)

Per trade, auto-capture: setup #, context, the level(s) hit + confluence count, the trigger (absorption/initiative, delta, SOS-SOW bar stats), entry/stop/target, R multiple, screenshot, and the data snapshot (VP levels, Weis wave, footprint at entry). That schema is what lets the 100-trade review retune the tools.

Glossary

Spring / UpthrustPhase-C false break of the range edge that causes the breakout — the tradeable shakeout.
TestQuiet probe (narrow range, volume < prior 2 bars) that doesn't break/launch. "No Supply"/"No Demand".
ClimaxThe Phase-A extreme that creates the range. Not a spring.
Creek / IceResistance / support edge of the range (from the AR + Phase B). Structurally an LVN.
VPOCVolume Point of Control — most-traded price; the control line.
VA / VAH / VALValue Area (68.2% of volume) and its high/low edges.
HVN / LVNHigh / Low Volume Node — acceptance (magnet, targets) / rejection (entries, stops, edges).
Absorption / InitiativePassive blocking (stops price) / aggressive push (moves price = the confirmation).
DeltaASK-aggressor volume − BID-aggressor volume. Rotation of sign = change of control.
Weis waveVolume summed per price swing — the effort meter that survives on tick charts.
SOS / SOW barSign of Strength/Weakness bar — wide range, close in the final third, high volume; the entry trigger.
