## Strategy Implications: Negative Gamma + High-Impact Catalyst = Defined Risk Required

The combination of a **negative gamma regime** and a **five-reading CPI print** is precisely the setup that punishes unhedged directional exposure and rewards defined-risk structures.

**What this means operationally:**

In a negative gamma environment, dealer hedging is **pro-directional** — they sell pressure further on declines and buy momentum on rallies. A CPI surprise in either direction will be amplified, not absorbed. Naked short premium (short straddles, iron condors) is inappropriate here; the regime mechanically works against the credit seller.
